# PS32 official requirements and implementation fit

Research date: 13 October 2026. The official HackSprint site https://hacksprint.sdgmitblr.club/ links to the organizer's Hack-Sprint listing on Commudle. Track 3 is Web3 & Cybersecurity. PS32 on https://www.commudle.com/communities/cdn-commudle-developer-network/hackathons/hack-sprint/tracks asks for a real-world Web3 problem or opportunity involving ownership, identity, trust or value. It allows broad technology choices and asks teams to challenge assumptions and demonstrate meaningful value and innovation. It does not require AI, DigiLocker, a specific chain, or a particular identity standard. No invented judging rubric is used.

Foliolet's hypothesis: an internship verifier needs a small typed eligibility statement, with issuer authority, holder approval and a reproducible current-state decision, rather than an entire marksheet. Tenant screening supplies a second income-evidence scenario. This is a product hypothesis, not validated customer demand or a proven unique invention.

Demonstration evidence: recognized test university can issue within its scope; rogue converter cannot; hidden fields are absent; holder signs exact disclosed commitments and verifier context; reader refuses wrong trust pins and stale/unavailable state; revocation changes current reliance while a saved decision retains its historical read context; application outage does not stop the reader when chain RPC remains available.

The organizers list 17-18 October 2026 as the event window. Treat credentials, institutions and governance in this repository as labeled test fixtures, not real affiliations.
