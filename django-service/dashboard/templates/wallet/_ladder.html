{# Assurance ladder (DESIGN.md §9). Expects `level`: SELF_ENROLLED | FIRST_SEEN_TRACKED | None (explainer). #}
  1. Self-enrolled

    The holder confirmed the facts. The commitment proves they were committed, not that they are true.

  2. First-seen tracked

    Enrolled bytes matched what the holder's own paired agent recorded earlier. This shows continuity, not that the issuer is genuine.

  3. Issuer-verified

    Not available in this version. It would require validating the issuer's signature on the source.
